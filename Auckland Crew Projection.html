Assumptions

  • Starting point: 216 rostered on 1 Oct 2026. The two 29/9 rows are read as two updates on the same day.
  • Current pace: about 10 a day since the 29/9 launch spike (+23, +12, +8 on the last three updates).
  • Oct: post-launch decay from 10/day to 6/day as the first wave of sign-ups is used up.
  • Nov: steady 5/day. 1–20 Dec: 4/day. 21 Dec–4 Jan: holiday slump at 1.5/day.
  • 5 Jan–1 Feb: pre-event surge ramping from 8/day to 16/day.
  • Low / High: base daily pace × 0.6 / × 1.4.
  • Agency: nothing allocated yet. Any shortfall at 1 Feb is the agency requirement unless recruitment pace improves.
